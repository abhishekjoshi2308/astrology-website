Meet Your Astrologer

Guidance rooted in tradition, delivered with clarity.

Acharya Surendar Shashtri was initiated into Vedic astrology at the age of fourteen under his grandfather's guidance in Ujjain, a city long regarded as a seat of Jyotish learning. Over the last decade, he has combined this classical training with a modern, practical approach — helping working professionals, families and business owners across India and abroad find clarity in moments of uncertainty.

His consultations draw on Parashari astrology, Nadi principles and Vastu Shastra, always explained in plain language rather than jargon. Every reading is treated with complete confidentiality, and every recommendation is meant to be genuinely usable — not vague or intimidating.

Today, Acharyaji consults with clients over video, voice and WhatsApp, and continues to teach a small circle of students the classical methods he was trained in.
